01 · Philosophy

The industry thesis

The friction

Startup support is built around the pitch and the funding round. Founders are coached to sound certain long before they have tested anything. The result: 90% of startups fail, and 75% of funded ventures fail, often because the first real stress test arrives after the money is spent.

Our thesis
Resilience is not a personality trait. It is the product of failing small, early and on purpose, and learning something true each time.
Non-negotiable principles
01

Test before you tell

No claim goes into a pitch, model or deck until it has survived a deliberate attempt to break it.

02

The problem is half the solution

A solution is a derivative of its problem. If the problem is vague, no amount of polish rescues the answer.

03

Teach the method, not the answer

Success means founders can run the process without us. Dependency is a failure mode.

04

Truth over comfort

We present unvarnished findings, including when they say pivot, pause or stop.

05

Stay local, think global

Ventures are scaled across borders but grounded in the ecosystems that raised them.

03 · Signature Framework

The Trial · Error · Truth Method

Our process, first run live as a four-hour "Hack Your Own Venture" intensive at Boston Tech Week, 2026, takes a venture through four phases. Each phase ends with an artifact and a decision.

PhaseKey questionWhat we doArtifact
1 · DiagnoseWhat is actually true today?Surface constraints and root causes. Locate gaps in product-market fit and go-to-market. State the problem concisely.Problem statement; assumption register
2 · ArchitectWhat must hold for this to work?Derive the solution from the problem. Build the value proposition as VP = f(P, S, A, ROI). Shape the business model around it.Value proposition; business model blueprint
3 · Stress-testWhat breaks first?Run conversion tests on the value proposition and model. Pitch under pressure. Rewrite until each claim survives.Test results; revised pitch script; pivot / persevere call
4 · GovernHow does this stay true?Install review cadence, metrics and partnership structure. Hand the method to the founding team.Operating cadence; partnership map; hand-off playbook

The core instrument: the value proposition formula

VP = Conversion → [ (Problem × Solution) → Advantage ] → ROI

A clear problem combined with an effective solution creates an advantage, which drives conversion, which results in ROI. The formula turns a vague slogan into a chain of claims that can each be tested. See the free working templates on the PythiaVox practice page.

05 · Concept Studies

How we think, shown on problems we haven't been hired for

These are illustrative studies applying the method to realistic scenarios. They are labelled as concept work and contain no client data.

Concept study · Scenario

The funded startup that cannot explain why customers convert

Context & challenge

A seed-funded B2B software company has an MVP and early users, but growth has plateaued. The team describes its value in features, and each sales conversation is pitched differently.

Strategic diagnosis

The problem statement is broad ("teams waste time"), so the solution has nothing to derive from. No advantage is defined, so conversion cannot be measured against it.

Proposed framework

Diagnose: narrow P to one customer and one costly task. Architect: derive S and A, then write VP = f(P, S, A, ROI). Stress-test: run the VP through ten buyer conversations. Govern: monthly review of conversion by claim.

Expected impact (hypotheses)

A single testable value proposition, a consistent pitch, and a clear view of which claim drives conversion. Success measure: improved conversion in the segment tested.

Concept study · Industry teardown

Why university tech-transfer programs stall between lab and market

Context & challenge

Programs such as university commercialization grants fund promising research, but many lab breakthroughs never become products. Researchers are expert in the science and new to market testing.

Strategic diagnosis

Funding is released before the commercial problem is stated. Mentorship is delivered as advice rather than as tests the project must pass.

Proposed framework

Add a Diagnose gate before funding tranches, a stress-test milestone with external buyers, and a governance hand-off so the research team runs the method itself.

Expected impact (hypotheses)

Fewer stalled projects, earlier kill-or-pivot decisions, and funding tied to evidence. To be validated against a program's own outcome data.
